This web application presents the main results from the study

D'Agostini M., Ventrucci M., de'Donato F. & Ranzi A. (2026) A Bayesian Spatially Varying Coefficient Model for Surface Urban Heat Island Analysis
The analysis focuses on the municipality of Bologna, Italy, and uses summer Landsat 8 land surface temperature observations from 2013 to 2025.

The maps display posterior summaries from a Bayesian spatially varying coefficient model fitted with the SPDE-INLA framework. The results include baseline land surface temperature, surface urban heat island intensity, local temporal trends, and posterior excursion sets identifying regions of statistically supported warming and cooling.
